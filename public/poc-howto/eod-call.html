POC will share the same updates from the 9:30 call with Execs, but in a shorter and more concise version.


Updates during the day may be included, for example when POCs are informed of newly onboarded resources, reach-outs, or deployment notices during the day.


The report should be significantly shorter than the 9:30 report.


Sequence

  • Burndown (Just say ahead/on-track/behind and provide a simple one-line overview. Avoid finer details.)
  • Dashboard (Just the count is enough. Details are not necessary.)
  • MyCompetency (Provide a simple overview.)
  • Attendance head count.

On Fridays, Group 5 is responsible for end-of-call picture taking.
